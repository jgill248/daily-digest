# Daily AI Digest — November 4, 2026 (Summary)

- **Oracle cancelled a $7 billion, 100,000-chip lease to Tencent under US pressure, the FT reports** — the five-year deal covered Oracle data centres in Southeast Asia, Guangdong officials separately pushed Tencent toward domestic chips, no party has commented and Reuters could not verify it, while Bloomberg says Chinese firms ordered about 6 million Nvidia RTX Pro 5500 cards worth $70 billion and will get roughly 1% within four months.
