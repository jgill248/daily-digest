- **Anthropic struck two major compute deals within about a day of each other**: a $9.1B, 20-year, 191-megawatt data-center agreement with Bitcoin miner Riot Platforms, and a new joint venture called Theseus with Macquarie Asset Management and GIC to build and lease dedicated data centers — its third major infrastructure commitment in three months, pushing cumulative commitments past $60 billion.

- **The enterprise "does AI spending pay off" debate got concrete answers from both directions**: Forbes profiled eight companies (Walmart, UPS, Bank of America, and others) with quantified AI ROI, while Fortune reported that "AI model routers" have become a hot new tooling category as companies try to tame surprise bills from long-running coding agents — and Manulife's CTO detailed a 30% developer productivity gain from its Microsoft AI partnership.
