# Daily AI Digest — August 28, 2026 (Summary)

- **Nvidia has agreed to buy Hugging Face for roughly $12.9 billion**, turning last week's reported "talks" into a deal, per The Information's reporting corroborated by CNBC and others — though neither company has confirmed it publicly.
- **A federal judge ruled the Pentagon acted illegally when it designated Anthropic a national-security "supply chain risk" in February**, finding the move was really punishment for the company's public criticism of the administration; Anthropic welcomed the ruling, and the government is expected to appeal.
- **Anthropic signed a $45 billion, six-year compute deal with UK infrastructure firm Nscale** for 460 megawatts of Nvidia Vera Rubin-powered capacity, landing as the company reportedly targets an October IPO at a $2 trillion-plus valuation on the back of $11.5 billion in Q2 revenue.
- **Anthropic previewed the Model Hardware Standard, a new protocol letting Claude directly operate lab and factory equipment** — microscopes, liquid handlers, robotic arms — with AWS, Danaher, Hugging Face, and Raspberry Pi as early testers; it plans to open-source the standard after the preview.
- **Salesforce's Agentforce crossed $1.5 billion in annual recurring revenue (up over 240% year over year)** and CrowdStrike posted its best trading day ever after a record quarter driven partly by AI agents becoming security customers themselves — two of the clearest earnings-report signals yet that enterprise AI spending is showing up in the numbers.
