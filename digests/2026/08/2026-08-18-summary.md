# Daily AI Digest — August 18, 2026 (Summary)

- **Nvidia will back OpenAI's Ohio data center with up to $105 billion in financing**, disclosed in a securities filing — the first project-specific deal to come out of the $500 billion Wall Street financing platform Nvidia announced with six asset managers on August 10, covering an initial 4.25 gigawatts of capacity (with an option for 3.75 more) coming online in phases starting 2028.
- **AI video startup Higgsfield raised $400 million at a $5.4 billion valuation**, roughly 4x where it stood six to eight months ago, led by DST Global with Goldman Sachs Alternatives and Intel Capital among the backers — the company says annualized revenue hit $700 million in August, up from about $20 million a year earlier.
- **Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B became the first local, on-device model to score frontier-class capability**, matching OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Luna on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index while running on a single consumer GPU — a step ahead of Meta's Muse Glimmer and Nvidia's Nemotron 3.5 Lightning in the same "runs on your laptop" category.
- **Grab cut mechanical analytics work from 44% to 30% of analyst tickets over five months** by routing routine data-prep, alerting, and reporting requests to AI agents under a five-level autonomy model, with cycle times down roughly a third — a rare case of a company publishing its own before/after operational metrics rather than a survey.
- **Claude suffered a 36-minute authentication outage on August 16**, taking down login for claude.ai, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, and platform.claude.com while the API stayed largely available; Anthropic restored full service in under 45 minutes.
