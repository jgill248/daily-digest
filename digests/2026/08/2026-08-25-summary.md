# Daily AI Digest — August 25, 2026 (Summary)

- **Hugging Face is reportedly in talks to be acquired for $13 billion or more** — nearly triple its 2023 valuation — though CEO Clément Delangue pushed back, saying the company is close to profitability and has no firm deal in place.
- **Nvidia is discussing a fresh investment in Perplexity at a valuation above $30 billion**, more than 50% higher than a year ago, extending Nvidia's strategy of funding both open-weight AI infrastructure (via a separate $6 billion Poolside deal) and consumer-facing applications that run on it.

- **Four health systems went live with Epic's real-time prior-authorization checks**, and Tempus AI won FDA clearance for software that flags undiagnosed pulmonary hypertension from a standard ECG — two narrowly scoped but production-grade clinical AI deployments, each with explicit limits on what the AI is allowed to decide.
