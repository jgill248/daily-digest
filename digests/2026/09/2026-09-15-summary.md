# Daily AI Digest — September 15, 2026 (Summary)

- **Apple shipped Siri AI in public beta on September 14, built on a custom Google Gemini model** — it ships free in English first, reserving the most capable features for iPhone 15 Pro or later with a paid iCloud+ subscription, and reaches an assistant installed on hundreds of millions of devices.
- **Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google DeepMind have quietly held working-group talks since July on a joint AI standards body**, per The Information, triggered by a Demis Hassabis proposal for FINRA-style pre-release testing, though the three labs reportedly still disagree on how much government involvement it should have.
- **Sam Altman told Fortune OpenAI won't IPO in 2026, calling it "an ill-advised moment" given safety concerns**, pointing to 2027 as more plausible without committing to a date.
- **Health systems at Becker's IT + Revenue Cycle Conference disclosed hard AI ROI numbers instead of pilot anecdotes** — Penn Medicine has budgeted $105 million in AI benefits through fiscal 2028, CommonSpirit generated over $100 million in FY2025, and Boston Children's Hospital reclaimed roughly 60,000 staff hours with OpenAI.
- **Meta began letting individual contributors in its ~7,000-person Applied AI division opt back into manager roles**, partially reversing the flat-org push central to Zuckerberg's "year of efficiency" earlier this year.
