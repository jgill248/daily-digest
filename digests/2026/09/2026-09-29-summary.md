- **Manus launched Cue, agents with their own phone number, wallet and computer, and Instinct raised $1 billion at a $10 billion valuation** — both bet that personal agents acting with real money and credentials are the next consumer category.
- **Oracle Health cites early results of a 33% cut in charge-lag days from its AI revenue-cycle agents** — the figure is vendor-reported from a secondary roundup, and the tools reach general availability only in the coming months.
