# Daily AI Digest — September 22, 2026 (Summary)

- **Meta's Muse AI agent topped 2.5 million downloads, and Amazon responded by blocking it from Amazon.com** — Meta declined Amazon's request to remove the agent, Shopify moved the opposite way by opening Shop Pay checkout to Muse, and Meta's stock jumped more than 11% on the growth.
- **xAI released Grok 4.7, a 2.1-trillion-parameter model partly trained on SpaceX engineering data**, at the same price as its predecessor; xAI's own benchmarks show gains on coding and long-horizon tasks, though the figures are self-reported and unaudited.
- **Google's open-source agent orchestrator AX shot to No. 1 on Hacker News**, offering Kubernetes-style primitives — Task, Workspace, Gateway, Model — for running AI agents in isolated, permission-limited sandboxes at scale.
- **Security researcher Patrick Wardle disclosed a zero-day in Meta's Muse that can hijack its microphone and credentials**, and recommended against installing the app until Meta ships a fix; Meta had not responded as of publication.
