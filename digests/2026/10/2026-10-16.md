## 1. Frontier Model News

### Meta released the weights of Muse Spark Open, an 816-billion-parameter model, on October 15
Meta put the weights of Muse Spark Open on Hugging Face and on Amazon SageMaker, Google Cloud and Microsoft Foundry, with hosted access through Groq, Together AI and OpenRouter. Reports describe an 816B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with 42B active parameters, a 1-million-token context window, text/image/video/audio input and text output, aimed at coding and agent work; Meta calls it the most capable US open-weight model to date and says it can run locally on two Apple M5 Ultra Mac Studios. This follows Meta's September teaser that Spark open weights were "coming soon" and its earlier Apache 2.0 release of the 30B Muse Glimmer; it is separate from the closed Muse Wonder launched October 12. *(Sources: SiliconANGLE, Unite.AI, Analytics India Magazine, PyTorch blog, Bloomberg headline, October 15)*

### The Muse Model License permits fine-tuning and redistribution, but with conditions
Per Trending Topics and Digitimes, derived products must carry a "Built with Muse Spark Open" label, companies selling the model as a hosted service need a separate agreement once they pass $20 million in gross revenue over 12 months, and military and critical-infrastructure uses are barred. Those terms make it open-weight rather than open source in the usual sense. *(Sources: Trending Topics, Digitimes, October 15–16)*

## Key Themes
